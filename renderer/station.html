⚙ CAR SETTINGS

Car information

Fuel & lap model (car defaults)

While the race clock runs, the tank drains continuously at the active burn rate (per-driver, or FCY when active) — laps only count tyre wear. Use SET in the fuel panel to correct to a real reading. Finish margin = fuel still wanted at the flag; safety level = fuel the tank must never drop below — all window and refuel calculations run on the fuel above safety.

Degradation & limits

Pit stop timing

Estimated stationary time = fuel ÷ refuel speed + tyre change, assumed sequential (no other work while fuel flows). Shown live in the stop planner and on the pit wall.

FCY / Code-60 time gain calculator

Every FCY lap, the field loses (FCY lap − green lap) versus green running — that is what a pit stop taken under FCY gains you compared to pitting under green.
